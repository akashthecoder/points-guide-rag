# Project Instructions

## Google Cloud and Google ADK teaching

Before implementing any non-trivial Google Cloud Platform or Google Agent Development Kit capability, teach the concept first.

The explanation must cover:

1. What the service or ADK component is and the problem it solves.
2. Its role in this project’s architecture and data flow.
3. Why it is preferred over the plausible alternatives for this milestone.
4. Important tradeoffs, operational costs, security/IAM implications, and common failure modes.
5. The smallest practical implementation step and how it will be verified.

Do not over-explain trivial configuration syntax. Link the relevant official documentation and add it to `reading.md` when it introduces a new concept worth studying. After a meaningful implementation, summarize what was built, why it works, the assumptions made, and what to learn next.

## Learning artifacts

- Record architecture and workflow decisions in `decision.md`.
- Update `flow.md` whenever the project’s data or execution flow changes.
- Keep `plan.md` milestone status and evidence current.
